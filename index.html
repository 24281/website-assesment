Classic Southern Fried Chicken

The gold standard of comfort food. This recipe delivers the perfect crunch—a savory, golden-brown exterior protecting meat that stays succulent and tender. It’s an iconic dish built on the perfect balance of garlic, pepper, and tradition.

Nashville Hot Chicken

Not just spicy, but legendary. This chicken is fried to a deep, dark crisp and then finished with a fiery, cayenne-infused hot oil paste. Served traditionally on a slice of white bread to soak up the heat, it’s a bold, smoky, and addictive experience for those who love a serious kick.

Crispy Buttermilk Tenders

A masterclass in texture, this recipe uses a tangy buttermilk brine to tenderize the chicken from the inside out. The result is an incredibly juicy, succulent bite encased in a signature light, shattered-glass crust. It’s the perfect balance of mild acidity and rich, savory seasoning.
